## Presenter script — approximately 35 seconds

Show the lunch screenshot for 15–20 seconds, then the care screenshot for 15–20 seconds.

> First, a woman with high blood pressure wants a practical lunch idea. femaktiv uses her saved report and food preferences to suggest a meal and explain its reasoning with sources.

## Preparation and provenance

The approved initial prompts were submitted through the existing live chat pipeline. Lunch needed one exchange. Care used one permitted follow-up to clarify the health insurer's role in temporary support without a care grade; its original answer remains in the saved history. The care screenshot focuses on the actual final clarification and answer. Generated replies were not rewritten, replaced or shortened by hand.

Preparation used **three user turns and six model calls** through Anymize. Observed turn durations were 17.16 seconds for lunch, 11.94 seconds for initial care, and 13.38 seconds for the care clarification. These are observed preparation timings, not promised response times.

Content was checked for consistency with the existing [DGE food guidance](https://www.dge.de/gesunde-ernaehrung/gut-essen-und-trinken/dge-empfehlungen/), [NHLBI DASH guidance](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/dash/following-dash), [NIH iron guidance](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/), [federal discharge guidance](https://gesund.bund.de/en/entlassung-aus-dem-krankenhaus), [care-advice guidance](https://gesund.bund.de/en/beratung-und-schulung), and [ZQP directory](https://www.zqp.de/beratung-pflege/). This review is not clinical validation.
